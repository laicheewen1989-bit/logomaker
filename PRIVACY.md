## English Version

LogoMaker ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our iOS application.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
LogoMaker is designed as a privacy-first, on-device creative tool.
* **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your personal data, contact information, or usage analytics.
* **On-Device Data Storage:** All assets created or imported by you—including custom photos, background wallpapers, design presets, draft names, and canvas configurations—are processed and stored strictly on your local device using standard iOS local sandbox storage (`FileManager` and `@AppStorage`).
* **No External Servers:** LogoMaker does not host or communicate with any external server to upload your user-generated content or design files.

### 2. In-App Purchases
All transactions and in-app purchases (such as LogoMaker Pro Lifetime Access) are processed securely through Apple's official **StoreKit 2** framework. 
* We do not have access to your credit card details, billing address, or Apple ID payment credentials.
* Transaction entitlements are verified directly with Apple's App Store servers locally on your device.

### 3. Third-Party Services
LogoMaker does not integrate third-party tracking tools, advertising networks, or analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Firebase). 

### 4. Children's Privacy
Our App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13, as no personal information is collected from any user.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

### 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:  
* **Email:** laicheewen1989@icloud.com

## 中文版本

LogoMaker（以下简称“我们”）高度重视您的个人隐私。本隐私政策旨在向您说明在您使用我们的 iOS 应用时，我们如何处理您的相关信息。

### 1. 信息收集与使用
LogoMaker 是一款基于本地离线设计的隐私优先工具。
* **不收集任何个人数据：** 我们不会收集、传输、存储或出售您的任何个人身份信息、联系方式或应用使用分析数据。
* **纯本地数据存储：** 您在应用内导入或创作的所有内容（包括自定义图片、背景壁纸、设计预设、草稿名称及图层配置），均仅保存在您设备的本地沙盒存储空间（通过 iOS 原生 `FileManager` 和 `@AppStorage`）中。
* **无外部服务器：** LogoMaker 不设任何外部服务器，不会将您的作品或照片上传至任何云端。

### 2. 应用内购买
应用内的所有购买操作（如 LogoMaker Pro 终身买断）均通过 Apple 官方 **StoreKit 2** 框架安全处理。
* 我们无法获取或保留您的信用卡信息、账单地址或 Apple ID 支付凭证。
* 购买凭证通过您的设备与 Apple App Store 服务器进行直接本地验证。

### 3. 第三方服务
LogoMaker 未集成任何第三方广告 SDK、用户行为追踪工具或分析组件。

### 4. 未成年人隐私保护
本应用不收集任何用户的个人信息，因此也不会收集 13 周岁以下未成年人的个人数据。

### 5. 隐私政策更新
我们可能会适时更新本隐私政策。任何修订都会在本页面公布，并更新生效日期。
