## English Version

If you have any questions, encounter issues, or need help with Xcode asset integration, please refer to the guide below.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: How do I import the exported `.appiconset` folder into Xcode?
1. AirDrop the exported `AppIcon.appiconset` folder from your iPhone to your Mac.
2. Open your Xcode project and click on `Assets` (or `Assets.xcassets`) in the Project Navigator.
3. Delete the default placeholder `AppIcon` entry.
4. Drag and drop the entire `AppIcon.appiconset` folder directly into your `Assets` catalog sidebar.

#### Q2: How do I restore my LogoMaker Pro purchase on a new device?
1. Open LogoMaker.
2. Tap the **Info (i)** icon or trigger any Pro feature (e.g., Export Xcode Asset).
3. On the Pro Paywall sheet, tap **Restore Purchases**. Ensure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

#### Q3: How do I move or resize the uploaded photo layer?
* Navigate to the **Layers** tab and enable **Photo Overlay Layer**.
* Drag your finger across the main preview canvas to adjust the photo position in real time.
* Use the **Photo Scale** slider to adjust its size.

#### Q4: Are the built-in SF Symbols free for commercial app releases?
Yes. All built-in vector symbols are based on Apple's official SF Symbols library. You may use them for application icons deployed within Apple platforms (iOS, macOS, watchOS, visionOS) in accordance with Apple's licensing agreement.

## 中文版本

如果您在使用过程中遇到任何问题，或需要关于 Xcode 资产集导入的帮助，请参考以下指南。

### 常见问题解答 (FAQ)

#### Q1：如何将导出的 `.appiconset` 资产包导入 Xcode？
1. 通过 AirDrop 将导出的 `AppIcon.appiconset` 文件夹传输至您的 Mac 电脑。
2. 打开 Xcode 项目，点击左侧导航栏中的 `Assets`（或 `Assets.xcassets`）。
3. 选中并删除原本自带的 `AppIcon` 占位选项。
4. 将整个 `AppIcon.appiconset` 文件夹直接拖拽入 Xcode 的 `Assets` 目录栏中即可。

#### Q2：换新手机后，如何恢复 LogoMaker Pro 的购买状态？
1. 打开 LogoMaker。
2. 点击右上角的 **信息 (i)** 图标，或触发任意 Pro 功能（如“导出 Xcode 资产包”）。
3. 在弹出的解锁页面底部，点击 **恢复购买（Restore Purchases）** 即可。请确保设备登录的 Apple ID 与购买时一致。

#### Q3：如何移动或调整相片图层的大小？
* 切换至 **图层 (Layers)** 面板，开启 **相片叠加层**。
* 在主预览画布上用手指直接拖拽，即可实时调整照片位置。
* 调节 **相片缩放** 滑块即可自由改变照片大小。

#### Q4：内置的图标可以用在正式上架 App Store 的应用里吗？
可以。内置矢量符号基于 Apple 官方 SF Symbols 库。根据 Apple 授权条款，导出的应用图标可合规用于 iOS、macOS、watchOS 等苹果生态的应用发布。
